PEDAGOGICAL ARCHITECTURE

The 5-Level NIPUN Bharat & Tholimettulu reading framework.

LEVEL 1 FLN-1

Aksharamulu

అక్షరములు

Single letter phoneme identification & vowel sounds.

Benchmark: 15+ letters/min
LEVEL 2 FLN-2

Sarala Padalu

సరళ పదాలు

Simple 2 & 3-letter words without diacritics or conjuncts.

Benchmark: 20+ WPM
LEVEL 3 KEY HURDLE

Guninthalu / Vottu

గుణింతాలు & వొత్తులు

Diacritic vowel markers & geminate / conjunct consonants.

Where 60% of students stall
LEVEL 4 FLN-4

Vakyalu

సరళ వాక్యాలు

Fluent syntactic reading in connected meaningful sentences.

Benchmark: 30–45 WPM
LEVEL 5 MASTERY

Katha

కథ & అవగాహన

Expressive story reading with listening & reading comprehension.

Benchmark: 60+ WPM
