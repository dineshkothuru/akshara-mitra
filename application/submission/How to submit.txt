================================================================================
KEY HIGHLIGHTS TO MENTION IF ASKED OR SHORTLISTED
================================================================================

  1. NIPUN Bharat & Tholimettulu Aligned:
     Covers all 5 FLN reading progression levels for Primary Grades 1 to 5:
     - Level 1: Aksharamulu (Letters & Vowels)
     - Level 2: Sarala Padalu (Simple Consonant-Vowel 2 & 3 letter words)
     - Level 3: Guninthalu & Vottulu (Diacritics & Geminate/Conjunct consonants)
     - Level 4: Vakyalu (Fluent connected sentence reading)
     - Level 5: Katha (Story reading & narrative comprehension)

  2. Dual-Tier Indic Speech Engine (Sarvam AI + Local Android Offline):
     - Primary Flagship: Sarvam AI Saaras Indic foundation model purpose-trained for Indian languages,
       capturing rural Telangana dialects, aspirates, and subtle conjunct consonants (ద్విత్వ/సంయుక్త అక్షరాలు).
     - Resilient Offline Fallback: Local Android on-device speech engine for 100% offline resilience in
       remote habitations without internet, zero cloud GPU costs, and strict DPDP Act 2023 minor data privacy.
     - Outperforms generic Western models (Whisper/Google ASR) which auto-correct student mistakes and fail
       diagnostic literacy evaluation.

  3. First-Class CwSN (Children with Special Needs) Inclusion under RPwD Act 2016:
     - Blind / Low Vision: Oral-Aural Auditory Phonological Mode + TalkBack screen reader support
     - Deaf / Hard of Hearing: Silent Visual Comprehension Mode (word-image association & sentence cloze)
     - Mute / Non-Verbal: Receptive Touch Literacy Mode & AAC Tap-to-Voice Sentence Builder (eliminates
       false "0 WPM" penalties and tests true cognitive decoding).

  4. Closed-Loop Remedial Drills:
     The moment a phoneme deficiency is identified, the system dynamically generates
     targeted flashcards and drills with native Telugu audio pronunciation guides.

  5. Teacher Diagnostic Desk & Bilingual Parent Slips:
     Classroom FLN distribution charts and one-click auto-generated parent progress notes
     in Telugu and English.

  6. Dynamic In-App Configurator & Zero Cloud GPU Cost:
     Teachers add custom stories and school U-DISE info with 100% offline LocalStorage persistence.
     Zero recurring cloud GPU bills for the department across 26,000+ government schools.
