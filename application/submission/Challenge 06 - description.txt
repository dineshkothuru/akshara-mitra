Problem: Foundational literacy (FLN) is Telangana's priority under NIPUN Bharat and Tholimettulu, but manual 1-on-1 reading tests take 8-10 mins per child. In multi-grade primary schools, teachers lose weeks of teaching time, feedback is delayed, and children with special needs (CwSN) are excluded.

Why generic tools fail: Western speech models auto-correct pronunciation, masking critical Telugu phonemic flaws like dropped vottulu (ద్విత్వ/సంయుక్త అక్షరాలు) and short/long vowel confusion. Standard voice apps also unfairly score mute or deaf students as zero.

Solution: Akshara Mitra is an autonomous, bilingual (Telugu & English) reading assessment studio and teacher diagnostic desk for primary Grades 1–5. Children read graded passages; the engine calculates real-time Words Per Minute (WPM), accuracy %, omissions, and hesitations across 5 FLN levels. When a gap is detected (e.g., dropping Pa-vottu in కప్ప), it dynamically generates targeted remedial drills with audio guides. Pioneers RPwD Act 2016 inclusive assessment: Oral-Aural mode for blind students, Silent Visual mode for deaf learners, and Receptive Touch & AAC Tap-to-Voice for non-verbal children. Auto-drafts bilingual parent progress slips.

Architecture: Dual-tier speech architecture—primary Sarvam AI Saaras Indic speech foundation model for state-of-the-art rural dialect accuracy, backed by an automatic local Android on-device engine for 100% offline resilience in remote schools. Strict DPDP Act 2023 compliance ($0 cloud GPU fees).

Stage: Operational, verified with automated test bench (7/7 passing tests).
